## Strategic Mandate

The program was executed as a technical enforcement initiative, not a policy-only review. Core mandates were:

1. Audit Asia environments against CIS Controls v8.
2. Identify control gaps, misconfigurations, and workflow weaknesses.
3. Enforce remediation at infrastructure and endpoint levels.
4. Produce documented proof for InfoSEC validation.
5. Align all Asia entities with the global security baseline.

## Centralized Platform Strategy

- Cross-region integration planning
- Policy alignment with enterprise standards
- Secure connectivity design
- Coordinated agent rollout and compatibility validation
- Zero-downtime enforcement across business operations
